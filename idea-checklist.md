## Steps for Getting Started

::: phasebox
**Phase 1 — Validate the drink concept**

- [ ] Visit 10+ comparable shops in DFW; note menu, pricing, rush-hour flow, and gaps
- [ ] Identify your signature angle (specialty coffee, boba, fresh juice, tea program, etc.)
- [ ] Run taste tests or pop-up samples with your target demo
- [ ] Define MVP menu (5–10 core drinks plus 2–3 modifiers or add-ons)
- [ ] Develop recipes and cost each drink (COGS per cup, including ice, milk, toppings)
- [ ] Test drink consistency and speed — can you hit target ticket time during a mock rush?
:::

::: phasebox
**Phase 2 — Build the drink program**

- [ ] Source core suppliers (coffee roaster, tea, boba/toppings, syrups, dairy/alternatives, cups/lids)
- [ ] Spec bar equipment (espresso machine, grinders, blenders, ice maker, refrigeration, hot water)
- [ ] Design bar layout for rush flow (order → prep → handoff; minimize steps between stations)
- [ ] Choose cup sizes, ice program, and to-go vs. dine-in packaging
- [ ] Plan cold storage and par levels for short-shelf-life items (milk, fruit, pearls, pastries)
- [ ] Decide on add-ons and upsells (extra shots, alternative milk, boba, seasonal LTOs)
:::

::: phasebox
**Phase 3 — Permits and build-out (beverage-specific)**

- [ ] Apply for food establishment permit (local health department — covers beverage prep)
- [ ] Complete food handler / certified food manager training (Texas requirement)
- [ ] If serving alcohol: TABC license research and application
- [ ] Confirm plumbing, drainage, and electrical load for espresso, ice, and refrigeration
- [ ] Finalize floor plan with health inspector in mind (handwash sink, sanitizer, temp storage)
- [ ] Pass health inspection (ice handling, milk storage temps, cleaning logs, sanitizer concentration)
:::

::: phasebox
**Phase 4 — Dial in and launch the menu**

- [ ] Hire and train lead barista on recipes, portioning, and consistency standards
- [ ] Dial in espresso grind, brew ratios, and blender times for every menu item
- [ ] Set opening/closing prep lists (batch syrups, cook pearls, calibrate grinders, restock bar)
- [ ] Run soft opening focused on drink quality, speed, and waste (not general business ops)
- [ ] Lock supplier delivery schedule for perishables before grand opening
:::
