Arlington

City of Arlington · Tarrant County

Research summary, not legal advice. Arlington has overlay districts and entertainment-zone rules near AT&T Stadium — verify parcel zoning with Planner of the Day.[4]

UTA / Entertainment District / South Arlington

Student and game-day traffic corridor. Mix of commercial, mixed-use, and overlay districts in Unified Development Code (UDC).[4]

  • Restaurant / food service — locate use in Nonresidential Use Table; permitted in most commercial districts Verify district[4]
  • Specific Use Permit (SUP): Required for uses not permitted outright — apply via zoning change process with owner signature and public hearing[4]
  • Entertainment District properties may have additional standards — meet with planner before lease (817-459-6502)[4]
  • Parking & landscaping: Enhanced requirements may apply adjacent to residential — UDC supplemental standards[4]

Permits & process — strict CO-first order

Arlington requires Certificate of Occupancy before Health Establishment Permit — opposite order from some cities' informal workflow.[1][3]

Health code (Article IV — Food Establishments)

  • Valid permit required for any food establishment, mobile unit, or commissary[5]
  • Separate permit for each operation at same address[5]
  • Plans review required for new construction, major remodel, change of ownership, or operation type change[5]
  • All commercial kitchens must hold current health permit[1]
  • Mobile food units: as of July 1, 2026, state DSHS permits required (HB 2844); city zoning still applies[1]

UTA-area strategy notes

  • Target walkable pads near campus with hours aligned to student peaks (afternoon and early evening)
  • Lease near existing restaurant CO shells to shorten change-of-use timeline[3]
  • Game-day/event parking restrictions in Entertainment District — confirm tenant parking count in lease
