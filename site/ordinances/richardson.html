Richardson

City of Richardson · Dallas & Collin Counties

Research summary, not legal advice. Check Zoning Atlas for special permits and planned development districts on each parcel.[2]

Telecom Corridor / office & retail pads

Richardson targets office lunch and commuter traffic. Base districts include C-M (Commercial), O-M (Office), LR-M (Local Retail), and numerous PD districts with custom use tables.[3]

  • Restaurant with food and beverage permit — listed use in CO application materials; confirm in Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance use table for parcel district[4][3]
  • Restaurant with drive-thru or curb service — may require Special Permit per Article XXII-A Special permit[4][3]
  • Restaurant without drive-thru — generally more straightforward in C-M and LR-M districts[4]
  • The Lone Volt counter-service without drive-through aligns with non-drive-thru restaurant classification[4]
  • Alcoholic beverages — special permit may apply depending on district and proximity standards[4]

Richardson-specific considerations

  • Office lunch daypart: Peak 11 AM–2 PM — zoning less restrictive than hours; lease and landlord rules may limit extended operation in office parks
  • Express Permitting: Qualifying small commercial projects get 3-business-day guaranteed plan review — ask Development Services if The Lone Volt finish-out qualifies[4]
  • Telecom Corridor aesthetics: Some PDs impose design standards on signage and facades[2][3]
  • Home food prep not allowed except Texas Cottage Food Law — all prep must be in permitted commercial space[1]
